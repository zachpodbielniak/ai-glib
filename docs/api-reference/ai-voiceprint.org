* AiVoiceprint and AiSpeakerMatch

The two values room mode passes around. Both are derivable GObjects. See [[file:../voice.org::*Naming speakers by voice][Naming speakers by voice]] for how they are used.

** AiVoiceprint

A person's name (or none, for an utterance not yet named), the embedding model that made it, and a unit-length vector. The vector is validated once, by the constructor: at least one value and at most =AI_VOICEPRINT_MAX_DIMENSIONS= (8192), every one finite, not all zero. Everything downstream can trust it.

- Voiceprints from two models are never compared: =ai_voiceprint_similarity()= returns -1 for them, and =ai_voiceprint_absorb()= leaves them alone.
- =ai_voiceprint_absorb()= is a running mean weighted by both counts; =max_count= caps the weight of the history so a voiceprint keeps adapting.
- =AiVoiceprintClass.similarity= is cosine similarity by default; a subclass may measure it differently.
- A valid name is 1 to =AI_VOICEPRINT_NAME_MAX= (64) bytes of UTF-8, with no control or format characters, =/=, =\=, =[=, =]= or =:=, not starting with =.= or a space or ending with a space, and not "Guest N".

** AiSpeakerMatch

Who said an utterance, how sure, and how that was decided.

| =AiSpeakerMatchKind= | Meaning |
|----------------------+---------|
| =AI_SPEAKER_MATCH_ENROLLED= | an enrolled voice matched |
| =AI_SPEAKER_MATCH_GUEST= | a voice nobody enrolled, numbered for the call |
| =AI_SPEAKER_MATCH_UNKNOWN= | a voice that matches nobody, not tracked |
| =AI_SPEAKER_MATCH_INHERITED= | too short to tell; whoever that microphone was last named (set by the session) |
| =AI_SPEAKER_MATCH_FALLBACK= | naming failed or timed out; the participant (set by the session) |
